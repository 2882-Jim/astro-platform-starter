DEMO SCRIPT: the full loop on one laptop (about 5 minutes)
==========================================================

Everything here is made up. The people, emails and passwords are not real.
Prototype only. A real version would use the firm's own login.

Before you start
- Use Edge or Chrome. Open audit-ai-suite-final.html (double-click it).
- If it opens on Home instead of "Sign in", someone has used it before:
  Home > TESTING ONLY box at the bottom > Reset everything > Yes, reset.
- The team code for this demo is:  DEMO-TEAM-CODE
  (In real use the manager makes a code on the Team tab and gives it out first.
  Any code of 8 or more letters works; capitals, spaces and dashes don't matter.)

Demo people you'll use (all on the master list):
  Jo Brown      Worker    jo.brown@example.com       password: jo-demo
  Aisha Khan    Senior    aisha.khan@example.com     password: aisha-demo
  Sarah Hughes  Manager   sarah.hughes@example.com   password: sarah-demo

1. Load the demo master
   - The screen says "This laptop isn't set up yet".
   - Click "Load demo master".
   - It says "Loaded 12 people (made up, for the demo)." and shows the
     "Demo people (made up)" table with every email and password.
   (Or: "Set up this laptop: load master list" and pick
    example-data/master-list.csv. It says "Loaded 12 people." The same people
    and passwords, but the table isn't shown.)

2. Sign in as the worker
   - In the table, click "Fill in" next to Jo Brown (or type
     jo.brown@example.com and jo-demo), then click "Sign in".
   - Home shows "Signed in as Jo Brown (Worker)" at the top right.
     There are 6 tabs and no Team tab: her role comes from the master list.

3. Log 2 reports
   - Click the "Heat map" tab.
   - What went wrong?  Bank total misread from the statement
     Area: Cash and bank.  Mistake: Wrong number.  Click "Log it".
   - What went wrong?  Invented a lease clause
     Area: Planning.  Mistake: Made something up.  Click "Log it".
   - "My reports" now shows the 2 squares.

4. Export with the code
   - Still on Heat map, scroll to "Send my data".
   - Team code: type DEMO-TEAM-CODE
   - Click "Export my data". It says "Saved as ai-suite-jo-brown-<date>.json,
     locked with the team code." The file goes to Downloads.
   - (Point out: open the file in Notepad and it's unreadable without the code.)

5. Sign out
   - Click the "Home" tab, then "Sign out" at the top right.
   - Back on "Sign in". It says "Signed out."

6. Sign in as the manager
   - Click "Fill in" next to Sarah Hughes, then "Sign in".
   - "Signed in as Sarah Hughes (Manager)". There's a 7th tab: Team.
   - (Point out: her Heat map is empty. Jo's reports stay with Jo, even on
     the same laptop.)

7. Create or enter the code
   - Click the "Team" tab.
   - Optional: under "Team code" click "Make a team code" to show how a
     manager makes one (shown once, never saved). It fills in the import
     box for you, but Jo's file used DEMO-TEAM-CODE, so:
   - In "Import team files", Team code: clear the box and type DEMO-TEAM-CODE

8. Import
   - Files from your team: click "Choose files" and pick
     ai-suite-jo-brown-<date>.json from Downloads.
   - Click "Import". It says "Imported 1 person."

9. Team view
   - Team view shows 1 person, 2 mistake reports, the team heat map and
     the People table (Jo Brown, teams Manchester, NQ1, from the master list).
   - "Show": pick Manchester (Jo is there), then London (no files yet), then
     All teams again. The line under the table says who hasn't sent a file.

10. Person view
   - In People, click "Jo Brown": her own map (Cash and bank / Wrong number,
     Planning / Made something up). Click a square to see its count.
   - Click "Back to Team".

11. CSV export
   - Click "Export team data". It saves team-data-all-teams-<date>.csv:
     counts only, never what went wrong. Opens in Excel.

Extras if there's time
- "Load demo team" (bottom of the Team tab) adds files from 8 more made-up
  people, so the team map and the Show dropdown look fuller.
- Master list box (Manager only): "Update master list" and pick
  example-data/master-list.csv. It says "Loaded 12 people." Loading a list
  replaces the old one. Passwords are scrambled before saving; the file
  itself isn't kept.
- Sign out and sign in as Aisha Khan (Senior): she can import and see the
  team, but has no team code, master list or CSV export.
- Wrong password: "Email or password not recognised."

To run the demo again: Home > TESTING ONLY > Reset everything > Yes, reset.
That deletes everything on this laptop, including the master list, so you
start again at step 1.
