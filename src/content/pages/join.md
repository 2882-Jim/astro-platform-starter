If you are training for the ACA with an ICAEW-authorised employer in Greater Manchester, MCASS is your society.

## How to get involved

1. **Follow us** on LinkedIn and Instagram to hear about events first.
2. **Come to an event.** See what's coming up on our Events page.
3. **Join the committee.** Elections are held every year. It's a great way to build your network and your CV.

Questions? Email us at [hello@mcass.uk](mailto:hello@mcass.uk).
