If you're training for the ACA with an employer in Greater Manchester, MCASS is your society.

## Getting involved

1. **Follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mcass_icaew/)** to hear about events.
2. **Come along to an event.** See the [Events](/events/) page.
3. **Stand for the committee.** Elections are held every year.

Questions? Email [hello@mcass.uk](mailto:hello@mcass.uk).
